# Quest 3S Port, Plan 2 of 3: The Game Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Super Mario 64 plays on the Quest 3S: the full room-lit renderer in Vulkan, the sm64 adapter booted from the user's ROM, Touch controllers driving Mario and the game's menus, and the level standing on the table that the headset's room scan knows, nudged with the grips and remembered.

**Architecture:** Plan 1 left a Vulkan device that renders the cube subset and an OpenXR platform with a fixed table and no input. This plan completes `IRenderDevice` (MSAA with resolve, stencil silhouettes, shadow/underside/footprint depth maps, the AO bake, composite, picture-in-picture, background pipeline warming) and the GLSL emitter (every room-lit feature), links the sm64 library for Android, and gives `QxPlatform` two real sources: Touch controller actions (`ar_pad` plus a pointer for the game's menus) and Scene-API table anchors with a grip-drag placer that reuses the portable placement record. `QxSession` grows to the parts of `VosSession::frame` that are not audio or foveation.

**Tech Stack:** as plan 1, plus OpenXR extensions `XR_FB_scene`, `XR_FB_spatial_entity`, `XR_FB_spatial_entity_query`, `XR_FB_spatial_entity_storage`, `XR_FB_scene_capture`, `XR_KHR_composition_layer_depth`; Vulkan `VK_KHR_depth_stencil_resolve` (optional), compute shaders.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-quest3s-port-design.md`. Plan 1: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-quest-1-foundation.md` (its Interfaces blocks are this plan's starting point).

## Global Constraints

All of plan 1's Global Constraints, plus:

- The Metal device's behaviour is the reference for every `IRenderDevice` method; where this plan does not say otherwise, do what `runtime/render/metal/metal_device.mm` does (the line numbers below point there). Formats: colour `R8G8B8A8_UNORM`, depth `D32_SFLOAT_S8_UINT`, depth maps `D32_SFLOAT`, AO volume `R8_UNORM` 3D. Sizes: shadow 2048², underside 256², footprint 512², AO maps 512² with up to 32 views.
- Stencil convention (`metal_device.mm:52-69`): bit `0x80` = depth written, bits `0x7F` = focus owner; modes Keep, MarkFocus (ref `0x81`), ClearFocus (ref `0x80`), Silhouette (Equal, read mask `0x7F`, increment-clamp), Faint (Equal, read mask `0x80`, write mask 0).
- Depth maps are forward-Z (clear 1.0, compare LESS); the main targets are reverse-Z (clear 0.0, compare GREATER_OR_EQUAL).
- Uniform blocks `ViewU` (160 B), `DrawU` (80 B), `LightU` (704 B) keep the Metal layout member for member (std140 rules give the same offsets for these vec4-aligned structs; assert `sizeof` on the C++ side).
- Input mapping to the game is `sm64_pad_from_ar` (`games/sm64/adapter/src/pad_map.c`): the game reads A, B (from B or X), Z (LB or left trigger), R (RB or right trigger), START, L (SELECT) and the left stick; the C buttons, R and L are masked by the adapter. The right stick is unused by the game.
- The sm64 adapter's requirements are the visionOS app's: `ar_adapter_sm64_set_resource_dir`, `set_pack_stamps_required(1)`, `set_bundled_pack_dir`, `ar_adapter_sm64_setup` before the game boots (`apps/visionos/vos_app.mm:82-114`, `:700-760`), the game started on its own thread by `ar_runtime_start_game_thread`.
- Scene permission: `com.oculus.permission.USE_SCENE` (manifest and runtime request). Anchor UUIDs are the Scene anchors' own; the app creates no spatial anchors.
- Development data location for this plan: the app's external files directory (`/sdcard/Android/data/org.pagetable.diorama64/files/`), pushed with adb (`rom.z64`, `res/{lang,dynos,palettes,mods}`, `packs/<name>/`). Plan 3 moves this into the APK.
- No sarec recording or replay on Quest; `GameSettingLock` is never locked.

## Review Focus

1. The room scan has no table, or the scene permission is denied: the app reports it (log line and a status string the panel will show), keeps rendering passthrough, and `get_table` returns 0 so the game clock does not run; it recovers without a restart once space setup is done. (Task 7 tests for the chooser with an empty list; on-device check.)
2. The scan has two tables: the nearest to the head is chosen, and "Re-place table" switches to another one when the player stands by it. (Task 7 `qx_choose_table` test.)
3. The player drags the level off the table edge: the offset is clamped to the table's 2D bounding box so the level never floats beside the table. (Task 7 `qx_drag_update` clamp test.)
4. A grip is held while the trigger is pressed: the drag and the game pointer never both act on the same ray; the drag wins and the pointer gets a cancel. (Task 6 `qx_route_pointer` test.)
5. A pipeline is refused (e.g. a sample count the device cannot rasterize): `take_pipeline_error` reports it, `supported_samples` drops to 1 and rendering continues at 1 sample. (Task 2 host test: `warm_pipelines` at a sample count above the device's records a refusal without calling Vulkan, mirroring the Metal device's `msaa_refused_`.)

  - `std::string pipeline_line(const PipeKey &k)` / `bool parse_pipeline_line(const std::string &line, PipeKey *out)`: the Metal format (`metal_device.mm:989-1038`) with the format field as the `VkFormat` integer; lines over 96 bytes or with fewer than 8 fields are rejected; at most 4096 lines are read by `warm_pipelines`.
  - `class WarmPool`: two worker threads; `enqueue(job)`, `claim(key)`, `collect(std::function<void(const Built&)>)`, `running()`, `peak()`, hold flags mirroring `hold_background_warm`, `hold_promoted_warm`, `hold_warm_compiles`.
- Device behaviour added: `supported_samples` returns 4, 2 or 1 from `VkPhysicalDeviceLimits.framebufferColorSampleCounts & framebufferDepthSampleCounts`, and 1 after any refused multisample pipeline (`msaa_refused_`). MSAA targets have transient (`LAZILY_ALLOCATED` when available) multisample colour and depth attachments with resolve attachments; depth resolves with `VK_RESOLVE_MODE_MAX_BIT` through `VK_KHR_depth_stencil_resolve` when supported, else `supported_samples` returns 1 for targets that need depth read-back (all of them: report 1 and log `AR_VULKAN depth_resolve=0`). Memoryless late-pass rule: a non-clearing first rec on a transient target drops the passes and counts `late_passes`. Stencil states per the convention; `VK_DYNAMIC_STATE_STENCIL_REFERENCE`, `DEPTH_BIAS`, `VIEWPORT`, `SCISSOR` dynamic; cull is in the pipeline key (three variants) unless `VK_EXT_extended_dynamic_state` is present. Samplers: the Metal cache key (linear, wrap_s, wrap_t, aniso16) with the smooth/vanilla filter rules (`metal_device.mm:1862-1881`); compare sampler linear/clamp/LESS_OR_EQUAL; `linear_clamp_`. Texture uploads retire the old image until `end_frame`; mips by `vkCmdBlitImage` chain, and when `set_fine_textures(true)` the gamma compute kernel with the 64 textures / 4 M texels per frame budget (textures over `kFineMaxTexels` use the blit chain). Three frames in flight with a semaphore-equivalent (fences) and three staging rings (1 MiB minimum, doubling, 256-B alignment, dropped when 4× over last use). `bind_target_texture` makes draws with that texture id sample the target's resolved colour (flushing the target first). `pipeline_list`, `warm_pipelines` (parallel on the pool, blocking), `warm_pipelines_background` (queued with `pipeline_siblings` as the Metal device computes them), `pipelines_warming`, `set_async_pipelines`/`async_pipelines`/`deferred_draws`/`take_deferred_miss` (the miss key format `slots/opts/bN/fN/dN/tN/cN/sN/kN`), `warm_compiles`, `warm_promotions`, `background_warm_peak`, `prepare_passes`. The `VkPipelineCache` is written to `pipeline_cache_path` at `end_frame` when new pipelines were built, at most once every 5 s.
